Minecraft-style voxel world rendered with a high-end ray-traced shader pack (BSL / Complementary Reimagined look). Everything is built from perfectly uniform 1x1 cubic blocks on a strict axis-aligned grid with sharp 90-degree edges - no rounded corners, no bevels, no subdivision, no organic curves, no smoothed silhouettes. Block textures are 16x16 pixel textures, crisp nearest-neighbour, visibly pixelated, never blurred or upscaled-smooth. Third-person over-the-shoulder camera, player character slightly right of centre, back to camera, mid-distance framing, horizon on the upper third. 16:9, 2560x1440. Volumetric god rays, soft shadows, subtle atmospheric fog with depth. Cinematic but clean - the centre of the frame stays calm and uncluttered, negative space in the middle third, so interface panels can sit on top of it.

CHARACTER: a blocky Minecraft player skin - black t-shirt with a single teal collar stripe, dark grey trousers, dark brown blocky hair, medium-brown skin, short beard. Flat cuboid limbs, no joints, no anatomy, no facial detail, no cloth simulation. Same skin, same proportions, same scale as every other image in this set.

SCENE: Wide plains biome at sunrise. Rolling grass-block hills, a small oak village of cobblestone and oak planks in the mid-left distance, a wheat farm with water channels, a few sheep. Warm low sun casting long horizontal shadows across the blocks, orange-to-pale-blue sky gradient, thin pixel clouds as flat white slabs. The middle and upper centre of the frame is open sky - leave it empty.

NEGATIVE: smooth surfaces, rounded edges, subdivision, bevelled cubes, anti-aliased textures, blurry pixels, realistic humans, real faces, hands with fingers, photorealistic skin, low-poly organic shapes, Roblox style, Lego studs, plastic sheen, toy bricks, text, watermark, logo, UI overlay, HUD, hotbar, hearts, XP bar, chat box, inventory, crosshair, motion blur, depth-of-field bokeh, fisheye, tilt-shift.
